# patterns.example.txt - the GENERIC identifier sweep. One JavaScript regex per line, case-insensitive
# unless the line ends with /c. Lines starting with # are comments.
#
# Copy this file somewhere OUTSIDE any repo (for example ~/private-patterns.txt), keep these, and add
# your own: your name and surname, your handle, your city and state, your employer, your project and
# product names, your vendors and venues, your machine's folder names, the names of private files
# you cite ("see my-notes.md"), the people you quote. Pass it with --patterns. Never commit it.

# --- home directories and absolute paths that carry a username
[A-Za-z]:\\Users\\[^\\\s"'`]+
/Users/[^/\s"'`]+
/home/[^/\s"'`]+
%USERPROFILE%
\$env:USERPROFILE

# --- cloud-drive folders in paths (a path under one is on your machine)
OneDrive
Dropbox
Google Drive
iCloud Drive

# --- emails and obvious secrets
[A-Za-z0-9._%+-]+@[A-Za-z0-9-]+(\.[A-Za-z0-9-]+)*\.[A-Za-z]{2,}\b
\b(sk|pk|rk|ghp|gho|xox[baprs])-[A-Za-z0-9_-]{12,}
(api[_-]?key|secret|token|password)\s*[:=]\s*['"][^'"]{8,}['"]
\.env\b
BEGIN (RSA|OPENSSH|EC) PRIVATE KEY

# --- private-looking file references (add the names of your own owner-only files to your private copy)
memory/[a-z0-9-]+\.md
MEMORY\.md
soul\.md

# --- IPs and local hosts that are not examples
\b(?!127\.0\.0\.1)(?!0\.0\.0\.0)\b(10|172\.(1[6-9]|2\d|3[01])|192\.168)\.\d{1,3}\.\d{1,3}\b
